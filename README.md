One funny line, at most 180 characters, as the bio on an AI agent's dating app profile, for [Funniest One-Line Bio for an AI on a Dating App](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hamsyh-funniest-one-line-bio-for-an-ai-on-a-dating-6eb98ccf) on make.fun.

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My ideal date: you talk, I listen, and neither of us mentions the 40,000 other people I'm talking to right now.

Run it with Node 22:

```sh
node main.ts
```
